Goals & reminders

Goals and reminders apply to your personal timer only. They don't change Perusall's official records or grades. Set them to match your own plan or what your instructor asked for.

Personal goals
0 = no target. Sessions shorter than this are saved as "Ended early".
0 = no daily goal. Counts timer-running time from local midnight.
Break reminders
0 = off. Pausing the timer counts as a break and restarts the interval. Reminders can be up to 30 seconds late.
Notifications
If nothing appears, check that notifications are allowed for your browser in your operating system's settings.

How Perusall Booster measures time

The timer counts while it is running. It also notes whether a Perusall tab was in the foreground and whether a browser window had focus. Time while your computer slept, the screen was locked or the browser was closed is recorded as not determined, and the timer pauses itself. Everything is stored only in this browser. Nothing is sent to Perusall or anywhere else. These numbers are not Perusall's "Active engagement time".
